About

I am drawn to work that begins with a question. My interest in communication is largely an interest in understanding — why certain messages land, how audiences form impressions, and what it takes to translate complex information into something people can actually use. Award 2023

Best Impromptu Speaker — Young Communicators Club, PRCI

Awarded at the Professional Relations Council of India's Young Communicators programme. Impromptu speaking is a different discipline from prepared communication — it tests whether clarity of thinking exists prior to, and independently of, preparation time.

The award is relevant not because of the recognition, but because of what it measures: the ability to organise thought quickly, identify the most important thing to say, and say it without the scaffolding of a prepared structure.

Beyond Work

Classical Arts

Bharatanatyam Visharad

I completed the Visharad examination — the advanced-level certification in classical Indian dance — under Akhil Bhartiya Gandharva Mahavidhyalaya Mandal in 2024. The Visharad is not a participation credential. It is an assessed examination at the conclusion of years of technically rigorous training, with formal evaluation of both technical proficiency and interpretive depth.

Reaching it required the kind of slow, corrective, standard-conscious work that does not permit shortcuts. That experience has had a practical effect on how I approach other domains: the willingness to work within a discipline's actual standards, rather than an approximation of them.

Intellectual Interests

Reading & Inquiry

My reading tends toward psychology, communication theory, cultural studies, and the history of ideas. The common thread is an interest in how people think, why they think it, and how that understanding can be made useful — which is, at a different scale, the same question that drives the research and communication work.

Occasionally I read fiction, but I find I am drawn to work that is precise about human psychology — novels in which characters think in ways that are recognisably accurate, rather than ways that are simply interesting. The distinction between the two is more significant than it might initially appear.
